# Cards

A container surface with slots for the patterns cards almost always need: a header (title, description, action), a thumbnail above it, and a footer beneath.

## Which one do I reach for?

- **[Card](?path=/docs/cards-card--docs)** — the default card. Pass header bits as props, body as children. Render as `<a>`, `<button>`, or `<article>` via the `render` prop when the whole surface should be interactive.
- **[CollapsibleCard](?path=/docs/cards-collapsiblecard--docs)** — a Card whose body folds away behind a chevron in the title, leaving the header and footer in view.

## Shared conventions

- The header renders when at least one of `title`, `description`, or `action` is set. The action sits in the top-right corner, and title and description text wrap beneath it once they clear it.
- The `footer` slot has no divider by default. Pass `FooterProps={{ separator: true }}` to draw a `Separator` above it.
- `color` takes a palette hue for the surface (`"neutral"` by default), and `shadow` lifts the card off the page.
- `variant` changes the surface treatment: `"ghost"` drops the background, border and shadow, and `"shell"` makes the card a page's main content area, full width with page-heading type and no border.
- Every slot has a matching `*Props` escape hatch (`HeaderProps`, `TitleProps`, `DescriptionProps`, `ActionProps`, `ThumbnailProps`, `FooterProps`, `InnerContainerProps`) for the rare case the slot prop isn't enough.
