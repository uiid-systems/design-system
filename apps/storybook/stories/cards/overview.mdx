# Cards

A container surface with slots for the patterns cards almost always need: a header (title, description, action), a thumbnail above it, and a divided footer beneath.

## Which one do I reach for?

- **[Card](?path=/docs/cards-card--docs)** — the default card. Pass header bits as props, body as children. Render as `<a>`, `<button>`, or `<article>` via the `render` prop when the whole surface should be interactive.
- **[CollapsibleCard](?path=/docs/cards-collapsiblecard--docs)** — a Card whose body and footer fold away behind a chevron in the title, leaving the header in view.

## Shared conventions

- The header renders when at least one of `title`, `description`, or `action` is set. The action sits in the top-right corner, and title and description text wrap beneath it once they clear it.
- The `footer` slot is preceded by a `Separator` automatically.
- Style variants (`inverted`, `trimmed`, `ghost`, `transparent`) compose with everything else — they only change the surface, not the slot layout.
- Every slot has a matching `*Props` escape hatch (`TitleProps`, `FooterProps`, etc.) for the rare case the slot prop isn't enough.
